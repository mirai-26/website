About MIRAI'26

A National Level Technical Symposium organized by the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Arunachala College of Engineering for Women.

ABOUT

What is MIRAI'26?

MIRAI'26 is a platform where innovation, creativity and technology come together. It provides students from various colleges an opportunity to compete, learn, network and showcase their talents through technical and non-technical events.

The symposium encourages participants to challenge themselves, exchange ideas and experience the latest trends in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.

VISION

Our Vision

To inspire students to innovate, collaborate and shape the future through technology, research and creativity.

MISSION

Our Mission

Learn

Provide opportunities to explore emerging technologies.

Compete

Encourage healthy competition and teamwork.

Innovate

Promote creative thinking and practical problem solving.

DEPARTMENT

About the Department

The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Arunachala College of Engineering for Women is committed to nurturing future innovators by providing quality education, practical exposure and industry-oriented learning.

Through workshops, symposiums, hackathons and projects, the department encourages students to develop technical expertise and problem-solving skills.

COLLEGE

About ACEW

Arunachala College of Engineering for Women (ACEW) is dedicated to empowering women through quality technical education. The institution provides excellent academic facilities, experienced faculty and an environment that promotes innovation and leadership.

ACEW strives to prepare students for successful careers while fostering ethics, confidence and lifelong learning.
